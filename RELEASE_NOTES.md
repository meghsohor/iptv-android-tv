The first release of MeghTV: a free, ad-free app for watching live TV channels on Android TV and Android phones.

## What you can do

- Browse thousands of channels by category or country, or search by name.
- Star channels to keep them in Favourites, newest first.
- Use it with a TV remote or by touch on a phone (landscape).
- On a TV remote, Channel Up/Down switches to the next channel in the list you picked from.
- Refresh the channel list at any time to get the latest channels.

If a stream doesn't work, MeghTV tries the channel's other sources automatically and marks channels that failed to play. You can delete channels you don't want; a refresh brings them back.

MeghTV doesn't host any channels. It plays streams that are publicly listed by the [iptv-org](https://github.com/iptv-org) project, so what's available depends on those sources.

## Install

Download **MeghTV-v1.0.0.apk** below and open it on your device. Android will ask you to allow installing apps from that source. You need Android 6.0 or newer. On a TV, an app such as Downloader can fetch and install the file.

**Used a test build before this release?** Uninstall it first. This version can't update it, and favourites from the old build don't carry over.
